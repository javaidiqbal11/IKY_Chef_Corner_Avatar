#Bacalao a la Vizcaína
Post Title: Bacalao a la Vizcaína

 Bacalao a la Vizcaína is a delightful embodiment of traditional Spanish cuisine, showcasing tender cod in a vibrant tomato sauce enriched with aromatic vegetables. This cherished dish not only highlights the rich flavors of the Basque region but also represents the heart of Spanish culinary culture. As you gently confit the cod and simmer the sauce, the delightful aroma will fill your kitchen with a warm, inviting atmosphere, perfect for family gatherings or festive occasions. 

 With each bite, the depth of flavor from the white wine, brandy, and choricero peppers will transport you to Spain, making this dish a true labor of love. Ideal for impressing guests or simply enjoying a taste of home, Bacalao a la Vizcaína is more than a meal; it's a celebration of culture and tradition that you'll want to revisit time and again. 

Instructions: In a casserole, heat garlic oil and add chopped white and red onions, leeks, carrot, tomato, and green pepper. Sauté gently over low heat for 1.5 hours, stirring occasionally.
After 1.5 hours, add torn stale bread, white wine, and brandy to the mixture. Allow it to simmer for another hour.
Blanch choricero peppers in boiling water for 2 minutes. Remove and scrape out the pulp, setting it aside.
Add fish stock to your onion-pepper mixture and simmer over low heat for another hour. Then, stir in the choricero pepper pulp and continue to simmer for another 30 minutes.
Remove from heat and pass the mixture through a food mill and fine sieve. Season with salt to taste, avoiding a blender to keep the sauce's color intact.
In another pot, heat garlic oil to about 80ºC (176ºF) without boiling. Gently add cod pieces and confit for 2-3 minutes until just cooked. Remove from oil and drain.
Place the confited cod in the casserole with the Vizcaína sauce, letting it finish cooking over low heat, ensuring the cod is covered with sauce for deep flavor absorption.
Serve your Bacalao a la Vizcaína warm with extra sauce on top for an authentic Spanish experience. Enjoy!

Instructions: In a suitable pot, pour enough high-quality olive oil to completely cover the desalted cod fillets. Add the whole peeled garlic cloves and a pinch of cayenne pepper.

Once the sauce has formed, return the pieces of cod to the pot and adorn them with the previously poached garlic cloves. Serve immediately to enjoy the rich, succulent flavors of this traditional Spanish dish.

 Indulge in the comforting layers of Eggplants Parmesan, a classic Italian dish that brings warmth and joy to any meal. This delightful recipe features tender, fried eggplant slices, rich tomato sauce, and an irresistible blend of cheeses, making it a harmonious blend of flavors and textures perfect for gatherings or cozy family dinners. The way the layers meld together creates a symphony of taste that’s hard to resist. For the ultimate experience, serve it warm with crusty bread or a fresh salad. 

Each bite is like a cozy embrace, showcasing Italian culinary artistry and simplicity. It's a fun dish to prepare, allowing you to immerse yourself in the cooking process while filling your kitchen with delightful aromas. Whether you're entertaining guests or just enjoying a comforting night in, Eggplants Parmesan is sure to delight and satisfy, becoming a beloved addition to your recipe repertoire. 

Instructions: Start by preparing the eggplants: peel them in a zebra pattern for added texture, then cut into 1 cm thick slices. Sprinkle them with salt and let them sit for about 30 minutes to drain excess moisture.
After 30 minutes, rinse the eggplant slices and pat them dry to remove moisture. Dredge each slice in flour, ensuring to shake off the excess. In a pan, heat sunflower oil and fry the eggplant slices until golden brown on both sides. Season lightly with salt and place them on paper towels to drain excess oil.
In a baking dish, start layering your ingredients like a lasagna: begin with a layer of fried eggplant, then add a layer of tomato sauce, followed by a mix of grated pecorino and parmesan cheese. Finish with a layer of fresh mozzarella slices. Repeat the layering process until you've used up your ingredients, aiming for at least two layers.
Preheat your oven to 180°C (356°F) and bake the dish for 10 to 15 minutes, or until the cheese is thoroughly melted and bubbly. For that extra golden crust, switch to the broiler for a few minutes towards the end.
Once it's done baking, let your Eggplants Parmesan cool slightly before serving. Enjoy this comforting dish warm, paired with some crusty bread or a fresh salad!
Ingredients: Eggplants | 3
Flour | 200 gr
Fresh mozzarella | 2
Grated Parmesan cheese | 400 gr
Grated Pecorino cheese | 200 gr
Tomato sauce | 700 gr | Sofrito

#Bocata de Calamares
Post Title: Bocata de Calamares

 Bocata de Calamares is a deliciously crunchy yet tender Spanish sandwich that will transport your taste buds straight to the streets of Madrid. This beloved street food combines the delectable flavors of fried calamari with the warmth of freshly baked bread, creating a culinary experience that's hard to resist. Topped with zesty lemon and a creamy homemade garlic mayonnaise, each bite offers a burst of flavor that’s perfect for any occasion. Enjoy it as a casual snack or a satisfying meal with friends and family! 

The joy of making Bocata de Calamares is also in its simplicity; it’s a fun recipe to whip up at home. From frying the calamari to creating the perfect bread rolls, each step is easy yet rewarding. Serve these delightful sandwiches at your next gathering or barbecue, and watch as they become the star of the show. Dive into this flavorful Spanish treasure and savor every moment of it! 

Instructions: To prepare the bread, combine 400 g of flour, 230 ml of warm water, 1 tablespoon of sunflower oil, 1 teaspoon of salt, and 4 g of fresh yeast in a bowl. Mix until you get a slightly sticky dough.
Let the dough rest for 5 minutes, then knead for 30 seconds. Repeat the resting and kneading for a smooth, elastic dough.
Cover the dough and let it ferment for 1.5 hours to rise.
Once the dough has risen, divide it into 4 pieces (about 165 g each) and shape them into rolls. Let them ferment for another hour.
Preheat your oven to 250°C (482°F). Make a long cut on top of the rolls and bake for about 20 minutes; first 10 minutes with steam, then without using a tray filled with water.
While the bread is baking, prepare the calamari. Cut 250 g of fresh calamari into thin strips and mix with the juice of 1 lemon and 1 teaspoon of salt to marinate.
After a few minutes, drain and pat the calamari dry. Dredge them in frying flour and fry in hot olive oil until they’re golden and crispy. Drain on absorbent paper.
For the garlic mayonnaise, microwave 2 sliced garlic cloves with a little olive oil for 2 minutes. In a blender, combine 2 eggs, sautéed garlic, a pinch of chili, the juice of 1 lemon, 100 ml of sunflower oil, salt, and pepper, blending while gradually adding 200 ml of olive oil until emulsified. Refrigerate.
To assemble, open the baked rolls, fill them with the crispy calamari, and drizzle generously with garlic mayonnaise. Serve immediately and enjoy!

Warm water | 230 ml
